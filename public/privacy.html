Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Aziz Electric ("we", "us"), Kot Road, Near Ayoub Chowk, Jhang Saddar, Punjab 35200, Pakistan, collects and uses information about its customers and suppliers.

Information we collect

When you buy from or sell to us we record your name or business name, phone number, address, and the details of your purchases, payments and account balance. This is needed to issue invoices and keep accurate accounts.

How we use it

We use this information to prepare invoices, quotations, delivery documents and account statements, to arrange delivery and payment collection, and to contact you about your orders.

WhatsApp messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may send you business messages through WhatsApp, such as invoices, quotations and account statements. We do not send promotional messages without your agreement. To stop receiving messages, reply STOP, or tell us by phone or at the shop.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with service providers we use to run the business (for example, messaging and hosting providers), or where the law requires it.

Storage and security

Records are kept in our own accounting system, with access limited to authorised staff, and are retained for as long as our accounts and legal obligations require.

Your choices

You may ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, by calling 047-7614881 or 0301-6295744, or by visiting the shop.
